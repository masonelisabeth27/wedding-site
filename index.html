Good to Know

FAQs

Everything you need to know before the big day.

When and where is the wedding?

Saturday, July 31, 2027, at Hawthorne House in Parkville, Missouri. The ceremony and reception will both be held at Hawthorne House. The ceremony begins at 4:00 PM, and the reception runs from 5:15 PM to 10:30 PM.

When should I RSVP by?

Please RSVP by the date printed on your invitation so we can finalize our numbers with the venue and caterer.

How do I RSVP?

Click the RSVP button on this site and search for your name. You'll be able to respond for everyone included on your invitation.

Can I bring a plus one?

Your RSVP will list everyone included in your invitation. If a guest has been included for you, you'll see them when you RSVP.

Are kids welcome?

We love your little ones! Children who are invited will be listed on your invitation and RSVP.

What is the dress code?

Cocktail attire. Late July in Kansas City is usually hot and humid, so lightweight fabrics are a great idea.

Will the wedding be indoors or outdoors?

Both the ceremony and reception will be held indoors. We'll also have outdoor activities on the lawn, so comfortable shoes that work on grass are a good idea.

What will the weather be like?

Highs in late July in the Kansas City area are typically around 90°F. Plan for warm weather and stay hydrated.

What airport should I fly into?

Kansas City International Airport (MCI) is the closest, about a 20-minute drive to Parkville.

Is there a hotel block?

We're working on hotel recommendations and a room block for out-of-town guests. Check the Travel section for updates.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We're having an unplugged ceremony. Please keep phones and cameras put away so everyone can be fully present. Our photographer will capture every moment, and you're welcome to take as many photos as you like at the reception!

Where are you registered?

Our registry is on Zola. See the Registry section for the link. Your presence is truly the greatest gift.

Who should I contact with questions?

Email us anytime at masonandelisabeth27@gmail.com. We're happy to help!
